## enum PairError

`Unresolvable` and `Model` are a case's outcome, written into its answer as
unresolved with the text as its reason; `Request`, `Internal`, and `Io` fail
the whole request. An overflow at any step is `Unresolvable` naming the
step, never a wrap, a panic, or a fall to float (Astra's review 12).
