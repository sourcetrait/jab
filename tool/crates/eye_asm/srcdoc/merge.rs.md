# merge.rs

The author's words are never the generator's to change: summaries, the
lines for arguments and results, a routine's signature, and a data label's
narrower type carry over. The code decides which symbols exist and their
order, every range, a macro's operand kinds, the results a macro still
writes, and its scratch. Nothing is reported: the difference between a stub
and its .eye, or git's after `--done`, is the report.

## fn macro_shape

Inputs the .eye places in fixed registers or memory, arguments with a
location, are not operands and carry over as written. An operand the author
listed as scratch stays scratch rather than becoming a result. A scratch
list naming the same registers keeps its text, so the author's order is not
replaced by the generator's.
