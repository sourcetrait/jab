# font.S

Generated by the kernel's `font` command, kernel/nu/mod/font, and its text,
comments included, is the generator's; the font.S committed names
kernel/font/spleen.nu in its header, the script that wrote it, until the font
is next generated. The console font is Spleen 12x24 (spleen-12x24.bdf from Spleen
12x24 2.2.0, BSD 2-clause, doc/thirdparty/spleen/LICENSE), each glyph 24
rows of 16 bits.

## font_glyphs

`2280 u16`: the console font's printable ASCII glyphs 32 to 126 in order, 24 rows each, the glyph in a row's high 12 bits.
