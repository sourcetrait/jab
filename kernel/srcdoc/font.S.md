# font.S

Generated by kernel/font/spleen.nu, and its text, comments included, is the
generator's. The console font is Spleen 12x24 (spleen-12x24.bdf from Spleen
12x24 2.2.0, BSD 2-clause, doc/thirdparty/spleen/LICENSE), each glyph 24
rows of 16 bits.

## font_glyphs

`2280 u16`: the console font's printable ASCII glyphs 32 to 126 in order, 24 rows each, the glyph in a row's high 12 bits.
