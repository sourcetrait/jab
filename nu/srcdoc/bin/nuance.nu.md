# nuance.nu

The workspace's nuanced verbs a host-test user runs himself, each one
call of an SDK module: the target's housekeeping (the campaign's decision
1). The justfile's `clean` and `retire` call them with the workspace's
directory.

## main clean

`build clean`: the workspace checked, then the whole target retired with
no path, else the outputs of the programs at or under it, or the
kernel's with `kernel`, in both trees or the one `--tree` names.
`--wrapped` passes `--tree` and its tree through as words.

## main retire

`build retire`: the workspace checked, then the retire home's tmp
deleted, the one deletion the tools make.
