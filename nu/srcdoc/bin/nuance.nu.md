# nuance.nu

The workspace's nuanced verbs a host-test user runs himself, each one
call of an SDK module: a bench's watch and the target's housekeeping
(the campaign's decision 1). The justfile's `watch bench`, `clean`, and
`retire` call them with the workspace's directory.

## main watch bench

`watcher bench`: with `<program>/<bench>` and the workspace last, the
bench's run in progress taken up, or its next waited for, every QEMU of
it recorded per thread under the step its command line names, and at
its end the bench's report and one per QEMU printed, `watch.nuon`
written, and the run packed again into the tar it names last; with the
workspace alone the benches listed.

## main clean

`build clean`: the workspace checked, then the whole target retired with
no path, else the outputs of the programs at or under it, or the
kernel's with `kernel`, in both trees or the one `--tree` names.
`--wrapped` passes `--tree` and its tree through as words.

## main retire

`build retire`: the workspace checked, then the retire home's tmp
deleted, the one deletion the tools make.
