# mod.nu

A pad table's check: the file's NUON held to
`nu/type/table/PadTable.nutype` by the SDK's `nutype problems` before a
launch plays it.

## def held

The table at `file` held to its model and its path returned, so a suite
case passes `--pad (padtable held <file>)` where it passed the path;
otherwise refused, `the pad table <file> breaks its model:` with every
problem the checker names. The SDK's launch reads the table itself.
