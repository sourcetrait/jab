# mod.nu

A gauge route's reader: the file's NUON held to `nu/type/route/Route.nutype`
by the SDK's `nutype problems` before any part of it is used.

## def read

The route at `file` read and returned whole once it holds to its model;
otherwise refused, `the route <file> breaks its model:` with every problem
the checker names, each a field's path and the type it wants. The gauge's
play reads its route through it before a run, and its report and compare
read a run's kept route through it after the route's SHA-256 holds.
