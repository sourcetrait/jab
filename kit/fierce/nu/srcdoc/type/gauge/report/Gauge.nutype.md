# Gauge.nutype

The shape of `gauge.nuon`, a batch's report (`nu/mod/gauge/report`'s
report-doc through its write: a route's runs, gauge play's host and
audio, and gauge read and read batch): its label, its one identity, the
ceiling, its runs, each with its effective identity, measurement, and CPU
windows, and its rows, one a frame; with or without the `reread` that
read batch adds. A run's corrections carry the sidecar's own values,
stated as text or null. The identities and the runs' records a read
copies from older captures are stated as their writers write them today,
so a report over captures older than those writers' changes holds the
shapes they wrote then. The workbench's landing holds a battery's reports
to it (jab-stress's `runner record outputs`).
