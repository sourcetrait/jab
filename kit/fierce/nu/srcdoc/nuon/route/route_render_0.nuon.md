# route_render_0.nuon

The gauge's route over Render Zero, the default of `gauge play route` and
the cadence bench's runs: normal play with no warmed cache, its legs
start, fight, spawn, yard, up, down, ramp, revisit, and pressure, the E at
56 s. Its header comment tells each leg. The file's bytes are the SHA-256
a run's identity records and compare groups by, so its comments stay in
it.
