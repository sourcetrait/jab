# route_settled.nuon

Render Zero's seven settled views as a route, played on the still copy
(`--map render_0_still`): each view placed, a bare L 300 ms later, then
held, a view's settled frames those after its leg's last frame building a
tile. Its header comment tells the views. The file's bytes are the SHA-256
a run's identity records and compare groups by, so its comments stay in
it.
